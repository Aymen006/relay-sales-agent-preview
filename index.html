What if Relay cannot answer a buyer?+

The conversation can go to your team with the buyer's question and order context so a person can reply.

Can I review changes to my order system?+

You can choose which updates need a person. Confirm the exact approval options for your connection.

Which channels and stores are available?+

Instagram, Messenger, Facebook, WhatsApp, Shopify, and CodPilot are shown as setup examples. Ask which connections are live for your account.

Can Relay use my store's tone?+

Your catalog, policies, and example replies can guide its answers. Ask to review sample replies before launch.
